# One hundred frontier encounters

Ten districts each contain ten saved street plans in arena_layouts.json. Houses use the roof/wall/door tiles from Kenney Desert Shooter Sample A, with side streets, intersecting sandy roads, central combat plazas, cover banks and peripheral props. Each stage saves its building footprints, roof style, door placement, cover and paths explicitly; collisions and rendering consume the same definition. Every open tile is connected to the player plaza.

Enemy navigation uses actor clearance and routes around whole buildings; projectiles require clear sight. The existing 100 campaign IDs, objectives, completion keys and rewards are retained. Gameplay balancing and native visual QA are recorded separately from structural connectivity tests.
