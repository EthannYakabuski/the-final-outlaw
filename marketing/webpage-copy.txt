The Final Outlaw
One outlaw. A hundred trails. A longer legend.

The Final Outlaw is a portrait top-down arcade shooter with 100 campaign challenges and an unlimited survival mode. Start with the first tutorial trails, then follow a winding level path through ten chapters of increasingly demanding encounters.

THREE SELLING CARDS
A trail worth following
Work through 100 campaign challenges across ten chapters, with clear, survival, supply, hold and guardian objectives.

Keep moving, keep your nerve
Drag to move, let auto-aim handle the next target, and dash clear when the desert closes in.

The frontier keeps going
Improve your gear and player level, then see how long your outlaw can hold out in unlimited survival.

TECHNICAL INFORMATION
Made with Godot 4.5.1 Compatibility. Portrait Android is primary; a Web build is provided where feasible. Local saves support offline core play. Android services have graceful Web fallbacks. Original code is Apache-2.0; source-asset/plugin notices are preserved. Google Play Games and production ads require the owner's final configuration.
