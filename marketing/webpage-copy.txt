One trail. A hundred last stands.

One trail. A hundred last stands.

The Final Outlaw is a portrait top-down frontier monster shooter with 100 campaign stages and endless survival. Fight through ten districts of handcrafted street plans: adobe houses, narrow alleys, road junctions and cover make each saved town a different encounter.

Move with your left thumb and hold the gun control to fire. Assisted targeting follows a visible foe, but every shot is your decision. Your starting magazine holds 20 rounds. Reload deliberately, watch the ammo counter and dash out of danger.

Fast fragile runners, heavy tanks, ranged attackers and guardians route around town obstacles. Clear encounters, survive the clock, gather supplies, hold your ground or defeat a guardian. Earn campaign stars, experience and equipment between attempts.

Seven permanent gear tracks improve damage, health, movement speed, magazine capacity, reload speed, dash recovery and armor. Daily quests and a seven-day gift streak offer more progress. Core play and progression save locally and work offline.
